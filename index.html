Why bother

Most free online tools are a form that uploads your file to somebody's server. That is fine for a holiday photo and a bad idea for a passport scan, a GPS track that starts at your house, or a contract.

These do the same work in JavaScript, in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, so there is nothing to log, leak, or subpoena. The counter above is live — it patches fetch and XMLHttpRequest and reports what actually happened, and it will turn red if any page here ever makes a request.

Keeping them

Every page is plain HTML with no dependencies. Save one to your desktop and it still works in ten years, with no internet connection and no build step. There is nothing to install and nothing to update.
